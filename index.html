Philosophy

Every workflow has scar tissue—legacy steps nobody questions, handoffs that lose signal, approvals that add delay but no value. Surgery is the discipline of seeing clearly and cutting precisely.

The SNAP Method

Scan

Map every step, handoff, and decision point in the current workflow. Identify where time, quality, and clarity are lost.

Narrow

Isolate the critical friction points. Rank them by impact and effort. Focus on what matters most, not what's easiest.

Act

Redesign the targeted steps. Remove, automate, or restructure. Every change must have a measurable outcome.

Prove

Measure the results against the baseline. Validate improvements with data, not opinion. Iterate or lock in.
